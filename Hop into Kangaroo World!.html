Super powers

Springy legs

Kangaroo tendons work like giant rubber bands. They store energy with every landing, so hopping uses less effort than you might think.

A strong tail

The tail balances the body while hopping. When a kangaroo stands still, it acts like a third leg.

Can't walk backwards

Their big feet and strong tails make going in reverse very hard. Kangaroos are built to go forward!

Pouch power

A baby kangaroo, called a joey, is tiny at birth, about the size of a jelly bean. It climbs into mum's pouch and grows there for months.

Life in the outback

Home

Kangaroos live in Australia and nearby islands, in grasslands, forests and dry deserts.

Food

They are herbivores. They eat grass, leaves and shrubs, mostly at dusk and dawn when it is cooler.

Mobs

A group of kangaroos is called a mob. Kangaroos in a mob look out for danger together.
